# Cycle 2: Challan #4 to #10 (plan for review)

Seven stories for the next cycle, posted every two days (about two weeks). Each one follows the rules set after
Challan #2's retention data:
- **30–40 s.** The first big number or space whip lands within 5 s.
- **No line runs longer than about 5 s** without a visual change.
- **Payoff first, explanation second.**
- **The subscribe ask is a ~1 s on-screen moment** (the radar gun's screen plus a callout), not a spoken beat.
  The pinned comment carries the full ask.

Every episode teases the next one in its last beat, so the cycle plays as a chain. #10 calls back to #2 (the weighing
machine) to close the loop.

Lines are in Roman Hinglish here for review; they get written in Devanagari for the voice engine at the voice stage.
**IS** = Inspector Sahab, **TW** = Tapri wala. Credits are estimates for one take per line (about 8 lines each).

- **Comment question:** "Suraj ki bijli se kitne ghar chal sakte hain?" It's open-ended, so expect fun guesses; the pinned comment can give a number answer.

- **Facts:**
  - Sun's power: 3.828 × 10²⁶ W.
  - World primary energy use: about 620 EJ a year (Energy Institute, Statistical Review of World Energy 2024).
  - One second of the Sun ÷ a year of humanity ≈ 617,000 years, so "kam se kam 6 lakh saal".
  - Hydrogen fused: about 600 million tonnes a second (NASA), so "saath crore ton" (shown 60,00,00,000+).
- **Credits:** about 450.
