# Plan: Challan #2 and #3, rebuilt in the Episode 1 3D style

Characters: Episode 1's models unchanged. **The guy is now called Tapri wala, and Hawaldar sahab is now Inspector Sahab** (names only).
Voices: Inspector Sahab = Ranga (the approved takes are reused). Tapri wala = **Bobby** (Episode 1's voice), one new take per line.
Look: Episode 1's worlds, whips, speed lines, 3D numbers landing on the spoken word, English captions coloured by speaker,
Roman Hinglish labels, and an end card in the "Sab ___ mein hain" style. Sound: Episode 1's whistle, radar and trombone,
plus whooshes, pops and booms made locally (no new sound credits).

Changes to the approved dialogue are **in bold**. Each one exists because the 3D world has no scooter, thana or poster,
or because a spoken number has to match the screen exactly.

## Cost and steps
- **Voice:** 12 Bobby lines plus 1 Ranga re-record (#2's c1) ≈ **1,050–1,150 credits**. Word timings with ElevenLabs Scribe cost 0. Other Ranga lines are reused.
- **Sound effects:** 0 credits (Episode 1's files plus locally made whooshes, pops and booms).
- **Build:** each episode gets its own copy of the Episode 1 project in `episodes/ep02` and `episodes/ep03`, reusing the scene code. New pieces: Moon and Jupiter worlds (#2); night sky, Earth–Moon orbit, laser and tides (#3).
- **Approvals:** a 720p draft of each plus one frame per scene, then wait for your OK before the 1080p final.

## Facts (unchanged from the approved scripts)
- Moon's surface gravity 1.62 m/s² ≈ 1/6 of Earth's, so 168 → 28 kg on the scale. Jupiter 24.8 m/s² ≈ 2.53×, so ≈ 425 kg. "400 se upar" → shown as 400+.
- The Moon recedes 3.8 cm/yr (lunar laser ranging off the Apollo 11, 14 and 15 retroreflectors). Tidal friction lengthens the day by about 2.3 ms per century (the net observed change is about 1.7), so "lagbhag 2" is shown as ~2.
