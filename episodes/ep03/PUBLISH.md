**Title:** Chand Pe FIR! 🚨 Har Saal 3.8 cm Bhaag Raha Hai #Shorts

**Description:**
Inspector Sahab ne CHAND ke khilaaf FIR likh di... kyunki chand sach mein faraar hai! 🌙☕

📏 Chand har saal lagbhag 3.8 cm Dharti se door ja raha hai
🔦 Kaise pata? Apollo astronauts chand pe mirror chhod aaye: laser maaro, roshni ke lautne ka time naapo, distance pakka
🌊 Wajah: tides (jwaar-bhaata). Dharti ka samandar chand ko aage kheenchta hai, aur uski orbit badi hoti jaati hai
⏳ Badle mein Dharti ka din lamba ho raha hai: 100 saal mein lagbhag 2 millisecond
