# Storylines

The YouTube original: a narrator over a zoom-out (chair, Earth, orbit, galaxy). Three re-imaginings:

1. **Challan (picked).** Device: a character does the work, plus a number lands. Form: a dialogue scene
   that the radar gun's beam blows open into the universe, then snaps back for the payoff.
   A cosmic traffic cop pulls over a guy sipping chai for "speeding". The radar gun proves it:
   1,670, then 107,000, then 720,000+ km/h. Then the cop points the gun at himself.
   Picked because it is a situation every Indian viewer knows, the joke lands on a real fact
   (everyone on Earth moves at the same speed), and the facts arrive as the cop's evidence, not a lecture.
2. **Speedometer.** Device: the object is the explanation. Form: one frame that rebuilds. The armchair
   has a car dashboard; the needle swings past each scale while the world outside the windscreen changes.
3. **The only still thing.** Device: life before and after, inverted. Form: match cuts. The chai in the
   kulhad is perfectly still while everything around it spins faster at every cut; the last cut shows
   the chai is moving too.
