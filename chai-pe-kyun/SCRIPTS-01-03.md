# Chai Pe Kyun? Episodes 1–3 (scripts for review)

Format (from `SERIES.md`, "Series ideas"): one everyday sky "why?" per Short, about 25 s. Big question on screen at
frame 0, a one-line answer, one simple visual, one thing to try yourself, a comment question, the next question as a
teaser, and a loop. Inspector Sahab asks (curious, not issuing a challan); the Tapri wala answers. Same voices
(Ranga for Sahab, Bobby for the Tapri wala). Lines are in Roman Hinglish for review; they go to Devanagari for the voice
stage. **IS** = Inspector Sahab, **TW** = Tapri wala. Captions on screen stay in English.

Each episode chains to the next, so the first three play as a set: blue sky → red sunset (same physics, one step
further) → the Moon following you.

## Episode 1: Aasmaan neela kyun hai? (about 25 s)
**Frame-0 text:** "AASMAAN NEELA KYUN?" (big, centred)  ·  **Cover:** blue sky + "NEELA KYUN?" sticker

| ~Time | Who | Line | Picture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | IS | "Arey, yeh aasmaan neela kyun hai? Paint kisne kiya?" | Daytime home, both look up at a bright blue sky. Question text on screen. |
| 3.0 | TW | "Paint nahi sahab, dhoop! Dhoop mein saare rang chhupe hain." | A white sunbeam passes through a prism and fans into a rainbow. |
| 7.5 | TW | "Hawa ke chhote-chhote kan neele rang ko sabse zyada idhar-udhar bikher dete hain." | Whip up into the sky: tiny air balls bounce blue light in every direction while red and yellow fly straight through. |
| 13.0 | IS | "Toh poora aasmaan neele se bhar gaya!" | Wide: the bounced blue dots fill the whole dome. |
| 15.5 | TW | "Ghar pe try karo: paani mein do boond doodh, torch maaro, side se neela dikhega!" | A glass of milky water, a torch beam: blue glow from the side, orange where the beam comes out. Text: "GHAR PE TRY KARO". |
| 20.5 | IS | "Chaand pe aasmaan kis rang ka hoga? Comment karo!" | To camera; ~1 s subscribe callout after. |
| 23.5 | IS | "Agla sawaal... shaam ko suraj laal kyun?" | The sky warms toward sunset (the teaser). |
| 25.5 | TW | "Sahab, upar dekho..." | Loops to "Arey, yeh aasmaan neela kyun hai?" |

- **Comment answer (pinned):** black! The Moon has no air, so nothing scatters the light. Apollo photos show a black sky
  even in daytime.
- **Facts:** sunlight is a mix of all colours; air molecules scatter short wavelengths far more than long ones
  (Rayleigh scattering), so scattered blue reaches us from every direction. Violet scatters even more, but sunlight has
  less of it and our eyes are more sensitive to blue. (Not said on screen; for replies.)
- **Try at home is real:** milk droplets scatter blue sideways, so milky water looks bluish from the side and
  orange-ish looking along the beam.
- **New visuals:** a daytime sky with a prism + rainbow fan, air "balls" bouncing blue dots, and a glass of milky water
  with a torch beam.

## Episode 2: Shaam ko suraj laal kyun? (v2 after the virality pass, 34 s)
**Frame-0 text:** "SURAJ LAAL KYUN?"  ·  **Cover:** "LAAL?" + "SHARMA RAHA HAI?" sticker

| ~Time | Who | Line | Picture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | IS | "Shaam ko suraj laal kyun ho jaata hai? Sharma raha hai kya?" | Sunset at home, big orange Sun. Question text. |
| 4.4 | TW | "Sharma nahi raha sahab, lamba raasta!" | Close-up, laughing. |
| 7.2 | TW | "Shaam ko dhoop tirchhi aati hai, toh hawa mein dopahar se lagbhag chaalis guna lamba raasta!" | Earth + air layer: noon beam straight down, sunset beam slanting; 3D **40** lands on "chaalis" (GUNA, dopahar se zyada hawa). |
| 12.3 | TW | "Aur neela sabse zyada bikharta hai, toh raaste mein hi utar jaata hai. Bas laal-narangi pahunchta hai!" | Blue dots peel off the beam, which fades white → red; an orange glow where it arrives. |
| 17.8 | IS | "Neele ka toh challan banta hai, beech raaste utar gaya!" | Snap home; radar reads CHALLAN!; tag "NEELE KA CHALLAN!" |
| 21.6 | IS | "Mars toh laal planet hai... wahan sunset kis rang ka? Comment karo!" | To camera; ~1 s subscribe callout. |
| 26.9 | IS | "Agla sawaal... chaand humare saath kyun chalta hai?" | Night falls; the Moon rises, then fades. |
| 31.3 | TW | "Dekho sahab, phir laal ho gaya..." | Back to the sunset; loops into the opening question. |

- **Why v2:** v1 was 36 s with 12 s of diagram, no number, no joke after the answer, a comment question without a
  wrong-guess setup and a loop line that went nowhere.
- **Comment answer (pinned reply):** blue! Fine Martian dust sends blue light forward around the Sun (Curiosity, Gale
  crater, 15 April 2015, NASA image PIA19400).
- **Facts:** at the horizon sunlight crosses about 38 times the air it crosses straight overhead (relative air mass ≈ 38);
  short (blue) wavelengths are scattered out on the way. Dust and smoke make sunsets redder.

## Episode 3: Chaand humare saath kyun chalta hai? (v2 after the virality pass, 34 s)
**Frame-0 text:** "CHAAND PICHHA KYUN KARTA?" over the night road  ·  **Cover:** "PICHHA?" + "HIMMAT TOH DEKHO!" sticker

| ~Time | Who | Line | Picture |
|---|---|---|---|

| 18.7 | IS | "Matlab chaand mera fan nahi hai?" | Home, disappointed. Tag "FAN NAHI HAI?!" |
| 21.0 | TW | "Aaj raat chalte hue dekho: ped bhaagega, chaand wahin rahega!" | "AAJ RAAT TRY KARO!" |
| 25.4 | IS | "Waise chaand khud kitni speed se ghoom raha hai? Guess karo, comment karo!" | To camera; ~1 s subscribe callout. |
| 30.7 | TW | "Sahab, chaand abhi bhi aapke peeche hai..." | Back on the road (the frame-0 shot), Sahab glances back; text "Agla sawaal: taare tim-timate kyun?". Loops. |

- **Why v2:** v1 opened on two people sitting, the hook didn't use the inspector, no joke after the answer, an easy
  yes/no comment question, a spoken teaser at the end (swipe-away point), 36 s.
- **Comment answer (pinned reply):** the Moon orbits at about 1 km/s, roughly 3,700 km/h, faster than a rifle bullet.
- **Facts:** the Moon's average distance is about 384,400 km. Nearby objects shift a lot against the background as you
  move (parallax); very distant ones barely shift, so the Moon keeps the same direction and seems to keep pace.

## Production notes
- **Voices:** about 7–8 lines per episode, roughly 250–300 credits each, about 850 for all three.
- **Length target:** 23–27 s. No silence before the first line; the question is on screen at frame 0.
- **Build:** the characters, home planet, tools/cycle2_patch.py and tools/cycle2_beats.py are reused. The beat
  generator needs one small change for this format: no trombone/kulhad/end-card beat (there is no challan). The
  "reversal" is replaced by the try-it-yourself beat.
- **Sources for the descriptions:** NASA Space Place "Why is the sky blue?"
  (https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/blue-sky/), NASA/JPL "Curiosity captures blue sunset on Mars" (May 2015; link to be checked
  before publishing), NASA Moon facts
  (https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/).
