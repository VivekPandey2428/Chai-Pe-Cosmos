**Answer key for replies (post the Mars answer as a reply the next day):**
- Mars pe sunset **NEELA** hota hai! 🔵 Wahan ki hawa mein bahut baareek dhool hai, jo neeli roshni ko suraj ke aas-paas
  aage ki taraf bhejti hai. NASA ke Curiosity rover ne 15 April 2015 ko Gale crater se ye neela sunset photo kiya tha
  (NASA image PIA19400).
  ⚠️ Before pasting a link: check the NASA/JPL page for PIA19400 opens ("Sunset in Mars' Gale Crater"); I couldn't reach
  NASA's sites from here to confirm the URL.
- "40 guna kaise?": horizon pe roshni seedhe upar ke mukaable lagbhag 38 guna zyada hawa paar karti hai (air mass ≈ 38).
- "Dhool/pollution wale din aur laal kyun?": dhool aur dhuaan aur zyada neela-hara bikherte hain, sunset aur gehra laal.
